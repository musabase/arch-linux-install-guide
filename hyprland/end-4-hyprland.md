# End-4 Hyprland on Arch Linux

End-4 is a visually rich Hyprland configuration that showcases
the graphical capabilities of the Hyprland Wayland compositor.

The setup focuses on modern design, smooth animations, and
carefully styled desktop components. It demonstrates how
Hyprland can be transformed into a highly polished desktop
environment while still maintaining the performance benefits
of a tiling workflow.

End-4 is often used by users who want to explore the visual
potential of Hyprland on Arch Linux.

---

# Why Use End-4

End-4 is useful for users who want:

- A visually impressive Hyprland desktop
- Advanced themes and animations
- A highly styled Wayland environment
- An example of what Hyprland customization can achieve
